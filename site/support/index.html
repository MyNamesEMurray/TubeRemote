Getting started

  1. Install TubeRemote on two devices that are on the same Wi-Fi network.
  2. On the device that will play videos, choose Viewer. On the other, choose Remote.
  3. Pair them once: tap Pair on the remote and check that both screens show the same six digits.
  4. On the remote, find a video and tap Send.

If the remote cannot find the viewer

  • Check that both devices are on the same Wi-Fi network and that the viewer app is open and on screen.
  • Check that TubeRemote may use the local network: Settings > Privacy & Security > Local Network.
  • Update TubeRemote on both devices. Different versions cannot talk to each other.

YouTube Premium and sign-in

Sign in on the viewer itself (menu > Browse YouTube), not only on the remote. The viewer plays the videos, so its sign-in is the one that counts.

TubeRemote Pro

Pro is a one-time purchase. If you bought it and it is not unlocked, open Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Pro on the viewer or on any one remote unlocks Pro features for everyone connected to that viewer.

Contact

For help or to report a problem, open an issue at github.com/MyNamesEMurray/TubeRemote/issues. In the app's Settings, tap the Build number five times to open a Debug Log. You can copy it and paste it into the issue; it contains no names or video titles.
