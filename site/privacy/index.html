Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

TubeRemote lets one iPhone or iPad control another. This page says what the app does with your information. In short: we do not collect it. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no TubeRemote server.

What stays on your devices

  • Your device name, the pairing keys for devices you have paired, your settings, your queue and history, and a short debug log. These are stored on the device. They are never sent to us.
  • If you turn on the saved queue, the remote keeps the last queue it saw, including the video that was playing and where it was, on that device.
  • The debug log lists events with no names, video titles or addresses. It leaves the device only if you copy it yourself.
  • Pairing keys are kept out of device backups.

How your devices talk to each other

Paired devices connect directly over your local network (Wi-Fi), using Apple's Bonjour discovery. After a device signs in with the keys made when you paired, what the viewer shows and the commands you send are encrypted. A device's name is visible to other devices on your network. Nothing passes through a server we run. TubeRemote asks for Local Network access for this, and only for this.

YouTube

TubeRemote shows YouTube's own website inside the app, and the viewer plays videos from it. What YouTube does with your information, including cookies and your sign-in if you choose to sign in, is covered by Google's Privacy Policy. The sign-in is kept on the device where you made it. TubeRemote does not read your password.

When you send a video, the app asks YouTube for its title and channel name, and loads its thumbnail from YouTube's servers. TubeRemote is not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube or Google.

Purchases

TubeRemote Pro is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment. We receive no payment details and no information about you. The app only learns from Apple whether the purchase has been made.

Sharing from other apps

The share option in other apps reads only the link you choose to share, and sends it to your viewer over the local network.

Children

TubeRemote is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect anything from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here with a new date.
